# Public Content Privacy

This context defines how personal writing is selected and transformed for
publication without sacrificing authorship or enabling avoidable profiling,
impersonation, or social engineering.

## Language

**Publication Decision**:
The binary result of reviewing content for a public audience: Publish or
Reject.
_Avoid_: Disclosure level, audience tier

**Publish**:
A decision that content may be made public unchanged or after permitted minimal
redaction.
_Avoid_: Pass, shareable

**Reject**:
A decision that content must remain non-public because it is unsafe, unsuitable,
or cannot be made safe without changing the original work.
_Avoid_: Restricted, private tier

**Information Facet**:
A non-exclusive description of what a passage contains, such as Expression,
Knowledge, Experience, Identity Anchor, Metadata Artifact, Exploit Primitive,
or Third-Party Information.
_Avoid_: Information type, category

**Subject Tag**:
The relationship between the information and its subject: Self, Public Persona,
Private Third Party, or Public Figure.
_Avoid_: Owner

**Sensitivity Band**:
A private S0-S4 classification of the strongest privacy concern in a passage or
post. It does not represent a public access level.
_Avoid_: Publication level, audience level

**Hard Gate**:
A condition that blocks publication regardless of other risk judgments.
_Avoid_: High score

**Inference Risk**:
The risk that protected information can be credibly deduced even though it is
not explicitly stated.
_Avoid_: Implied detail

**Marginal Corpus Risk**:
The additional identification, linkage, relationship, timeline, or targeting
capability contributed by one post to the existing public corpus.
_Avoid_: Standalone risk, aggregation score

**Minimal Redaction**:
A localized removal or `[redacted]` replacement that preserves the original
argument, structure, voice, style, chronology, and emphasis.
_Avoid_: Rewrite, sanitization

**Stylometric Linkage**:
The possibility of linking writing across identities or sources through
language and style.
_Avoid_: Identity proof

**Editorial Value**:
The enduring expressive, experiential, technical, cultural, or creative value
that justifies republishing a legacy post.
_Avoid_: Popularity
